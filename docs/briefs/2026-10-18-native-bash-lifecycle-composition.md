## Intended result and exact gap

The bounded workset and single-segment automatic-discovery source components,
plus typed Actor completion consumption, have local source/documentation approval.
They are uninstalled and V18 remains FAILED. The remaining native-protocol gap
is the coexistence of complete ordinary foreground Bash task metadata, automatic
foreground-to-background transitions, and the previously approved strict native
multi-segment lifecycle. The original validator rejects foreground metadata;
its genuine thirteen-event probe alone cannot prove an ordinary mixed stream.

Support only fully bound known-producer Bash lifecycle composition. Preserve
every actual init and result in its original order, every required intermediate
result and final clean terminal, original native flags and actual numeric exits.
Do not introduce a repeated-init/no-intermediate-result admission or a general
late-held-result protocol. Retain the original approved lifecycle validator
byte-identical; new composition must be explicit version2 evidence, not a claim
that the original validator accepted a different raw stream.

new path, including ordinary scheduled execution rather than protected cases
alone. Existing scheduling, permissions, budgets, models and timeouts stay fixed.

original dictionaries in both original and projected evidence. Admit only known
