# Pricing skill: full economic reasoning and independent exercise

## Problem

A correctly loaded pricing project was not reliably used to construct offers.
The workflow could copy proposed price rows, overlook daily production capacity
and omitted costs, promise unsupported capabilities, or defer known answers.
Its strict approved-price wording also conflicted with operator-requested drafts
intended for commercial review.

## Change

Rewrote the pricing skill template around explicit cost groups, capacity and
supplier breakpoints, a separate commercial pricing decision and verification
of the actual customer deliverable. The workflow reads the shared project's
operator corrections and model contract; company values remain outside the
kernel. It distinguishes draft proposals from send/reuse approval and preserves
engine-owned drafting, dossier, source-order and no-send boundaries.

Logistics use full gross shipment scope; development, trials and routine quality
control stay distinct; unavailable capabilities cannot be offered as R&D.
Missing facts trigger focused verification, not a generic founder handoff.

## Validation

Skill frontmatter validated. 34 templates across 3 fixture configurations render
successfully. No calculator or runtime code changed. An authorised fresh worker
with no forked conversation completed a realistic read-only offer exercise.
It used capacity, supplier thresholds, scope-specific costs and concrete
proposals; development/freight remained explicitly unresolved. The instance
evaluation record preserves the prompt, predeclared rubric, report, calculations
and limitations. No email was sent or draft persisted during the test.
