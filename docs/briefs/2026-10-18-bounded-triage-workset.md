# Bounded, resumable ordinary triage

V18 created the real owner-bound Café draft and received the MrCall reply, but
the Café operator exhausted its 45-minute allocation before a native terminal.
Its retained stream contains 99 Bash calls and three background evidence agents:
18 assignment reads, 17 dossiers, 11 contacted reads, 11 histories and 23 thread
reads. Discovery and review each reached the native ten-minute foreground limit.
The actual Café cost state reports USD4.0855942 against a soft USD4 setting.
The failed result remains unsigned; successful provider effects do not certify
the unfinished ordinary route or the native continuation protocol.

## Intended result

Keep the entire ordinary discovery inventory and every held thread visible,
while selecting at most two ordinary conversation revisions for deep triage in
one scheduled tick, including task-discovered candidates and draft replacements.
Selection is symbolic, owner-bound and resumable; it neither
answers mail nor grants assignment authority. Fresh inbound work gets a bounded
newest-first opportunity; a fair persisted reservation order services older work.
Existing exact-thread assignment, dossier, history, dedup and contextual generation
checks still run for each selected conversation before any effect.

## Scope and design

- Add a company-neutral workset module and focused offline/subprocess tests.
- Collect the complete ordinary task inventory, draft review and discovery before
  selecting the tick's shared workset. Task-only exact roots and draft re-decision
  roots participate in the same ordinary queue and fairness policy; they cannot
  escape the two-root limit or remain permanently outside its input. Missing
  exact identity remains an explicit held item, never a guessed conversation.
- Attach a stable conversation revision to ordinary open/resumed rows, derived
  from exact inbound/provider identities and current assignment routing metadata.
  Bind same-run task/review observations to the existing private health context
  before selecting the combined workset in scheduled discovery. Interactive
  inventory reads do not reserve work. No extra live reads are introduced: reuse
  the actual recurring inventory commands in the documented order.
- Store only owner-bound revision hashes and reservation order in a private local
  cursor beside the existing state database. Repeated use of one run nonce and
  inventory is idempotent. Corrupt, foreign, unsafe or inconsistent state fails
  closed with explicit incomplete workset evidence, never unrestricted triage.
- Emit selected and deferred exact roots without dropping or relabelling the full
  inventory. Reservation is scheduling, never answered/handled/closed evidence.
  A failed tick may rotate reservations, but every still-pending revision remains
  eligible in later ticks. New inbound identities create a new revision.
- Scheduled skill guidance deep-triages only selected roots, preserves held work
  from the fresh complete discovery, and starts no evidence subagents or waiting
  loops. Unselected task-only work, deep draft reads, replacement generation and
  redrafting are explicitly deferred with their identities and reasons; deferral
  is not resolution or successful processing. Read-only draft verdict inventory
  remains complete. All remaining recurring phases still execute and report
  actual results. Non-mail campaign authority is unchanged and cannot turn a
  deferred mail item into a reply through another surface.
- Protected originals use their existing admission boundary and the same ordinary
  ordering; the selector does not inspect fixture labels, nonce, expected answers,
  sender domains or company literals. No test-specific priority is introduced.

## Acceptance and limits

Offline checks prove bounded selection, deterministic ordering, fair resumption,
new-revision admission, owner/context binding, idempotency, unsafe-state refusal,
full inventory/held-work preservation and unchanged action guards. Retained V18
inventory is a private regression input, not a live acceptance substitute. Existing
recovery, discovery, assignment, rendering and full kernel checks must pass.

This bounded delivery permits no additional model/provider calls, paid retry,
budget/deadline increase, engine change, installation, release or retrospective
PASS. Native auto-backgrounded discovery and the interrupted four-init/zero-result
stream remain separate proof obligations: this workset must not weaken their
validators or claim that a successful final native case is already established.
Only free evidence that addresses remaining composition blockers can justify
proposing another separately bounded ordinary acceptance.

## Rejected alternatives

Do not increase the timeout, hide held/UNKNOWN rows, narrow discovery to fixtures,
mark reservations handled, trust cached authority for effects, merely prompt the
model to be faster, or start more agents. The root selection and reservation are
mechanical; the engine remains responsible for grounded generation.
