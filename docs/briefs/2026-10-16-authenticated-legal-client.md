# Direct authenticated legal workflow client

## Intent

The authenticated operator explicitly authorizes legal research and rejects
repeated identity/scope forms. The engine candidate now exposes protocol 2 with
workflows.interactive.start. The existing thin clone client requires protocol 1
and manual enrollment, so it cannot use that candidate.

This decision supersedes only the enrollment and three-confirmation requirements
in the 2026-10-15 clone workflow bridge brief. Owner authentication establishes
the principal; a yes/no response cannot establish physical human presence.

## Scope and constraints

Adapt the company-neutral persistent legal client and its canonical three-host
thin routing skill to the existing direct-start contract. Preserve explicit
public queries/URLs, same-connection continuation, EOF cancellation, private
artifact integrity, old-engine refusal and no local/generic fallback. Known
scheduled/customer-mail/read-only contexts remain denied by the engine. Research
grants no mail, filing, payment, publication or company-memory authority.

Do not change authentication implementation, permissions, scheduled templates,
full local legal procedures, installed clones, service processes or release pins.
No retirement, deployment, paid engine trial, commit, push or release is included.
The original native, operational and retirement gates remain open separately.

## Acceptance

Actual controlled WebSocket and terminal/JSON CLI journeys start and continue
protocol-2 legal work without enrollment or confirmation input. Reject protocol-1
and malformed capability responses before start. Verify exact query inputs,
same persistent socket, waiting/responses, EOF cancellation, completed downloads
and absence of automatic reconnect/replay. Three rendered host paths and copy
fallback expose identical updated thin instructions; installed package resources
match source. Retain a real source/integration review and documentation closure.
These controlled responses do not establish fresh paid/native or clone acceptance.
