# Source-bound business read outcomes in ordinary native health

## Intent and observed defect

V21's ordinary MrCall run creates and sends the exact selected fixture draft,
retires its one verified Gmail mirror and has one genuine successful native
terminal. Independent read-only provider verification finds exactly one correct
reply. Its supervisor nevertheless refuses a foreground task notification for
an optional assignment-status read that returned a typed human-verification
hold. That read's entire RFC component is disjoint from the sent fixture root;
the original fixture projection and exact draft handoff are retained separately.
V21 remains FAILED; Café/signup did not run and no retrospective PASS is allowed.

The CLI deliberately reports some successful business observations with nonzero
codes. Native task metadata records these as failed tasks. Health must recognize
the exact source-backed observation without turning human-held work into action
authority or accepting real technical failures. The complete read-contract audit
is retained externally as read-outcome-contract-audit.md in the recovery workset.

## Scope and constraints

Cover the finite audited read-business outcomes: canonical complete discovery
with human holds (already protected), exact assignment-status JSON human holds
with exit3, and complete contacted/history absence with exit1. Missing selected
scope, unknown/malformed/foreign/contradictory authority, unreadable mailboxes,
incomplete tasks/review inventories and technical errors remain failures.
Scheduled rootless assignment status remains a scope refusal. All failed or
uncertain actions and mutations remain failures, including draft/send/retirement.

Use source-owned typed evidence tied to actual current owner, exact requested
root/contact, exact query/window/argv, invocation nonce, actual CLI/native process,
original tool/task/session and exact output bytes/code identity.
Plain text, a substring, a code alone or a model summary cannot certify a business
outcome. Reuse existing strict projection/hold semantics and protected transport
primitives where they fit. Preserve original native events and exit statuses;
no conversion of failed metadata to success, cached action authority, extra
upstream reads, engine deployment, public CLI flag/exit/stdout change or model.
Private evidence is bounded, owner/run-bound and cannot certify another action.

Preserve company rules, binary/driver pins, mode/send permissions, authentication,
budgets, deadlines, cron, pause semantics, protected drafts and excluded engines.
The installed root-bound compose/exact-send route remains unchanged. The lead
owns this kernel/Actor component; unrelated concurrent source remains untouched.

## Acceptance

1. Original immutable V21 events replay as a valid native lifecycle only when
   every known business read is independently bound and validated; all original
   literal values and terminals remain unchanged. Historical V21 stays FAILED.
2. Shared producer/consumer/health semantics agree. Mutated owner/root/contact,
   task/session/nonce, missing or contradictory proof, technical UNKNOWN and
   failed actions refuse. Required discovery/inventory/phase guards stay strict.
3. Real local subprocess tests cover finite business outcomes and negative
   boundaries without models/provider writes. One broad source suite plus
   necessary affected checks establish integration; no ritual repeats.
4. Fresh reviews precede implementation/adoption; both normal installed imports,
   exact producer/source pins, controls and scoped free checks pass independently.
5. Documentation closure distinguishes source/installed evidence from unmet
   ordinary/provider acceptance. No new paid case, public release, fixture send
   or cleanup of existing customer drafts is authorized by this brief.

The umbrella recovery remains open until separately authorized operational proof
succeeds. No broader read-status exception is implied by this finite repair.
